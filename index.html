How to play

⚡ Myths & Facts challenge

First time someone draws one: read this out loud!

  1. Read the statement out loud.
  2. Answer “True” or “False” in English and explain why in one sentence.
  3. Tap your answer: the app gives the right answer, a short explanation and its source.
  4. Right answer → random bonus. Wrong answer → random penalty.
  5. The Myths & Facts card is then out of the game.
